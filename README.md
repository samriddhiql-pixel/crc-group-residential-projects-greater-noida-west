What Should Homebuyers Compare Before Choosing a Project?

The BHK configuration is only one part of the decision. A detailed comparison should include several practical factors.

Apartment layout: Look at room dimensions, circulation space, balconies, utility areas and storage rather than relying only on the stated BHK category.

Carpet area: Compare usable space between shortlisted apartments and understand exactly what is included in the quoted area.

Amenities: Consider facilities that your household is likely to use regularly. A large amenity list is less important than having facilities that match your lifestyle.

Location: Travel time to workplaces, schools, hospitals, shopping areas and other frequently visited destinations can have a significant impact on everyday convenience.

Project documentation: Review RERA registration, approved plans, specifications, payment schedules and applicable charges before proceeding.
